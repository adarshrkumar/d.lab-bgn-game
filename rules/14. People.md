## People

### Robber

The robber will take 1 gold from you (-1 gold)

If you do not have gold, the robber will take 1 food (-1 food)

### Swampman

You've been taken captive by the evil Swampman (he was nice to you earlier to gain your trust).

He says that you may leave after 5 turns or by paying him 3 gold.

Since you are in his dungeon you cannot do anything at all including fishing.
