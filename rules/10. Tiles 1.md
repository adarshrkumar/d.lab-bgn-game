## Tiles

### Forest Tiles (Food)

Landing Bonus: **\+2 food**
Terrain Bonus: **None**

#### Explore for Forest: (Roll a die)

1. Get [robbed](#robber)
2. \-1 health
3. Your next turn, you cannot move or explore.
4. \+1 bow
5. \+2 arrows
6. \+1 food

### Mine Tiles (Gold)

Landing Bonus: **\+1 gold**
Terrain Bonus: **None**

#### Explore the Mine: (Roll a die)

1. \-1 health
2. Your next turn you cannot move or explore.
3. \+1 health
4. \+1 gold
5. \+1 upgraded Sword Lvl.
6. \+1 food

### Mountain Tiles (Bows)

Landing Bonus: **\+1 arrow**
Terrain Bonus: **Increased bow range (See [Combat](#combat))**

#### Explore the Mountain: (Roll a die)

1. Get [Robbed](#robber)
2. \-1 health
3. \+2 arrow
4. \+1 Bow
5. \+1 upgraded sword lvl.
6. \+1 food
