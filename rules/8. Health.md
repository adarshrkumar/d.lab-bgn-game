## Health

- Each player starts off with 5 health.
- You may gain health through Exploring and loose health through Combat
- You can heal yourself as an action.
- If you have no food to consume, you lose 2 health.

### Poison

Getting poisoned makes you startled and run away to the previous tile that you were on.

It also forbays you from returning to that tile for 4 turns.

### Death

- If you die, respawn on a start tile with the starting inventory (See [Setup](#setup))
